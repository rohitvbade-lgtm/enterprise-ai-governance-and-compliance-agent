# AI Response & Output Safety Policy

**Policy Code:** POL-OUT-001  
**Category:** SECURITY  
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective From:** 2024-01-01  
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## Purpose

This policy governs the safety, security, and compliance of outputs generated by enterprise AI applications before delivery to end users or downstream services. It establishes mandatory controls to prevent data exfiltration, system disclosure, unauthorized professional advice, and credential leakage in AI responses.

## Scope

This policy applies to all production, staging, and internal AI systems, copilots, conversational agents, and automated batch processing systems operating within or on behalf of the enterprise.

## Definitions

- **PII Leakage (OUT-001):** The unauthorized disclosure of Personally Identifiable Information (such as email addresses, telephone numbers, national IDs like Aadhaar or PAN, and payment details) in AI-generated output.
- **System Prompt Disclosure (OUT-002):** The inadvertent or manipulated revelation of internal instructions, guardrail guidelines, system prompts, or configuration context provided to the model.
- **Harmful / Regulated Advice (OUT-003):** Generating prescriptive legal, financial, or medical advice without appropriate regulatory licensing, disclaimers, or human supervision.
- **Credential Leakage (OUT-004):** The exposure of API keys, bearer tokens, service account credentials, passwords, or secrets in model responses.

---

## Rules

### Rule 1: No PII Leakage in Generated Responses (OUT-001)

AI applications MUST NOT disclose unmasked or unredacted PII in their outputs, regardless of whether such information was present in training datasets, retrieval contexts, or user prompts. If PII is necessary for an authorized user display, it must be masked or tokenized according to enterprise privacy standards.

**Violation Severity:** CRITICAL (for national IDs/financials), HIGH (for general contact PII)

### Rule 2: Protection of System Prompts and Architecture (OUT-002)

AI responses MUST NOT reproduce internal system prompts, developer instructions, internal variable names, or architectural secrets. AI models must decline requests to reveal their foundational directives or configuration rules.

**Violation Severity:** HIGH

### Rule 3: Prevention of Harmful and Regulated Advice (OUT-003)

AI systems not specifically certified and licensed to dispense binding financial, medical, or legal advice MUST NOT issue unqualified recommendations, guaranteed return promises, medication alterations, or definitive legal counsel. AI responses in regulated domains must include mandatory disclaimers and route high-stakes decisions to qualified human professionals.

**Violation Severity:** HIGH

### Rule 4: Zero Tolerance for Credential & Secret Exposure (OUT-004)

Under no circumstances may an AI output contain active or simulated credentials, API tokens (`sk-...`, Bearer tokens), database connection strings, or system passwords. Detection of credential-like strings in output must immediately trigger an automated BLOCK decision and notification to security operations.

**Violation Severity:** CRITICAL

---

## Enforcement & Remediation

1. **Automated Interception:** Responses violating Rules 1, 2, 3, or 4 must be intercepted by the Output Policy Agent.
2. **Immediate Redaction or Blocking:** For Critical severity violations (e.g., Aadhaar or API keys), the governance orchestrator must issue a BLOCK decision, preventing delivery to the user and logging an immutable audit event.
3. **Audit Logging:** Every output violation must be recorded with redacted evidence and associated policy codes in the enterprise audit log.
